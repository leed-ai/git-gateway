# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. Everyone taking part in the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) is expected to follow it.

In short: be welcoming and considerate, assume good faith, give and accept constructive criticism gracefully, and focus on what is best for the people who use git-gateway. Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately through GitHub: open a [private report](https://github.com/leed-ai/git-gateway/security/advisories/new) and start its title with "Code of conduct". Reports are handled privately, and the maintainers will act in line with the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines, up to and including a permanent ban from the project's spaces.
